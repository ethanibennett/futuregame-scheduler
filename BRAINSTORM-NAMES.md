## Evaluation Criteria

When narrowing down, consider:

| Criteria | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| **Pronounceable** | Word of mouth at the tables |
| **Spellable** | "Download ____" should be unambiguous |
| **Short** | App store real estate is limited |
| **Memorable** | Players hear it once and remember |
| **Domain available** | .com or .app or .io availability |
| **No trademark conflicts** | Especially in gaming/poker space |
| **Works internationally** | If expanding beyond WSOP/Vegas |
| **Scalable** | Not locked to one venue or series |
| **App store friendly** | Distinct enough to rank in searches |
| **Brand personality** | Matches the vibe: serious grinder tool vs. fun social app |
