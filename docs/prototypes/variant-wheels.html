        Variant Wheels

        Two wheels read as one phrase: the betting structure on the left, the game on the right. A game with relatives has an arrow; tap it and a third wheel slides out to the right with its variants. Hold'em opens to Pineapple and Short Deck, Razz to Action Razz, Razzdugi, Razzdeucy and 2-7 Razz.

        Try it

        • Flick or drag either wheel, tap a row, or focus a wheel and use the arrow keys.
        • Land on Hold'em or Razz and tap the arrow beside the game. Pick a variant on the new wheel, or tap the arrow again to fold it away.
        • Change the game and the structure wheel turns to that game's usual structure. Turn it yourself to log an unusual one (Pot Limit Hold'em, No Limit Razz); the line under the wheels says when you have.
        • Short Deck, PLO5, PLO6 and PLO6 8/b are played one way only, so the other structures are struck through and the wheel springs back.

        What changed from direction 9

        • Structure is its own wheel, first. No Limit, Pot Limit, Limit: three rows, always in the same place.
        • The game wheel lists 14 games, not 48, named as families where it has to (2-7 Draw opens to Triple and Single Draw). Everything else sits one level out, in its game's variant wheel, so a flick covers the whole list and there is no family rail or More row.
        • Variants open rightward, as you asked for Hold'em and Razz. I applied the same pattern to the other families with relatives; the table below marks which ones are your call and which are mine.
        • Mixed games (HORSE, 8-Game) and the bottom-of-screen sheet from direction 9 are left out of this round, so the wheels can be judged on their own.
