Swiss, second pass.

The first pass got the materials right: Helvetica, black, white, one red. These are fifty ways to make it Swiss in its structure as well, drawn from the grid manuals and posters of Zürich and Basel. Each has a specimen, the screens it touches, a size, and a box to tick.

What the first pass does

Thin and bold Helvetica, red for selection and action, ink bands for venues, no radii or shadows, hairline graph lines. It still keeps the app's old skeleton: outlined boxes around every control, centred labels, a red outline for a selected chip, chevrons centred under cards, and an italic series line. Most of what follows replaces that skeleton with typographic structure.

The first pass's screenshots were rendered on Windows in Arial, which has no thin weight. The specimens here use Helvetica Neue where your device has it, so on a Mac or iPhone the thin weights show as intended.
