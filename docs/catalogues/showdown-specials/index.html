Showdown Specials

A hundred ways to mark a showdown the hero wins with something special. Each card plays its idea on a small table, on a loop. Pick by number; tap Pick to collect them and copy the list.

  • No card ever moves: every idea plays on the cloth, the rail or the room around the table.
  • Nothing blinks, flashes or pulses. Things fade in over 120ms or more and stay steady.
  • Every idea runs on the replay's springs, so Back, a jump and a video export all show it right.
The demos are sketches of the idea, drawn on a toy table: timings, colours and sizes would be set on the real felt, in g and r, and measured there. "Fits" is a suggestion; any idea can be tied to any hand.
