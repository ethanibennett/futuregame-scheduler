Showdown Specials

Forty ways to mark a special showdown, each built from the hand itself: the five cards that play, their suits and ranks, the outs, the pot in big blinds, who lost and where they sat. Every demo plays on a copy of the replayer’s table. Another hand deals a different hand that earns the same idea, so you can see it change.

  • No card ever moves. Everything plays on the cloth, the rail or the room around the table, under the cards.
  • Nothing blinks, flashes or pulses: lights rise and settle once, and fades run 120ms or longer.
  • Each one would run on the replay’s springs, so Back, a jump and a video export all show it right.
The demos are drawn on a copy of the replayer’s 6-max landscape table with real hands; the cards that do not play are dimmed, as the replay dims them. Timings, colours and sizes would be set on the real felt, in g and r, and measured there. “Fits” is the hand each idea was built for; most could be tied to another.
