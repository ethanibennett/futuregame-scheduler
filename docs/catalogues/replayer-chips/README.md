# Replayer chips: 20 alternative designs

20 proposed designs for the replayer's 3D chips (wagers on the felt, the pot, the chips that fly when the pot ships). Nothing has been built: every picture is a standalone mockup at phone scale and enlarged, on the dark felt. "Today" (what the app draws now) is described at the top of [proposals.md](proposals.md), with a capture from the app: [00-today-app.jpg](00-today-app.jpg), and drawn the same way as the options: [00-today-drawn.jpg](00-today-drawn.jpg).
Each row has a full write-up in [proposals.md](proposals.md) (and `items.json`). `gallery.html` is the whole catalogue as one self-contained page.
